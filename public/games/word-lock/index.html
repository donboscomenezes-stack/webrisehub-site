About Word Lock · How to play

Play Word Lock free online. Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries, choose Quick Play or Daily Word, and solve the puzzle using letter clues.

Play Word Lock – Guess the Five-Letter Word

Find the hidden five-letter word in six attempts. Each guess must be a valid word. Quick Play gives you another puzzle to solve; Daily Word offers the shared daily challenge. Take one puzzle at a time rather than rushing your guesses.

Letter clues and controls

A check mark means the letter is in the right place. A dot means it belongs elsewhere in the answer; a cross means it is absent. Type on your physical keyboard or tap the on-screen letters on a touchscreen. Use Enter to submit and Backspace to correct your guess.

Beginner tips

Start with a word that checks several different letters. Keep confirmed positions in mind, test letters you have not ruled out, and remember that an answer can repeat a letter. Read the feedback after every guess before using another attempt.
