About WebRise Farm · How to play

Play WebRise Farm – A Cozy Farming Game Online

Grow crops, raise animals and decorate a cozy 3D farm in WebRise Farm. Play the free farming browser game and save or share a farm photo.

How to play WebRise Farm

Name your farm and select Start Farming. Use the toolbar to prepare soil, plant seeds, water crops and harvest them when ready. The starting farm gives you crops and a farmer so you can learn by trying the tools. Fill orders to earn coins and experience, then explore the barn and shop.

Crops, animals and decorations

Balance planting and harvesting with caring for your animals. Spend the coins you earn on the choices offered in the shop. Place decorations to make the farm your own, rotate items before confirming their position, and let your farmers help with the work.

Controls and devices

Use a mouse or supported touchscreen to choose tools and work on tiles. Drag to move the view and use the mouse wheel or a two-finger pinch to zoom. A desktop browser offers more space; touch input and portrait camera adjustments are also implemented. The 3D scene needs a browser and device capable of WebGL.

Create a farm photo

Open Photo mode to choose a camera angle, time of day, visual filter and frame. Take a photo, review the result, and use Save photo or Share where your browser supports it. Saving a photo keeps an image of the farm, rather than a playable saved game.

Saving and progress

Your farm lives in the current tab. There is no persistent farm save or account: reloading starts a new farm. Take a photo before leaving if you want a keepsake. Offline play has not been verified for this release, so load the game with an internet connection.
