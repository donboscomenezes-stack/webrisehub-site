About Firefight · How to play

Play Firefight – A Free 3D Browser Shooter

Play Firefight, a free 3D browser shooter with five maps, customizable bot matches and a choice of weapons. Learn the controls and start a battle.

How to play Firefight

Choose a map, your main weapon and match settings, then enter a free-for-all against bots. Set a kill target or a time limit and choose a bot difficulty that gives you room to learn. Bot matches support a total of 2 to 16 fighters, depending on the map and settings.

Default controls

Use W, A, S and D to move, the mouse to look, left click to fire and right click to aim. Shift sprints, Space jumps and C crouches. Press R to reload, E to pick up a dropped weapon, and 1 or 2 to switch between your main weapon and pistol. V changes perspective, Q swaps shoulders, and Tab shows scores. Press Escape to release the mouse before opening this guide. Controls can be remapped in the game settings.

Weapons and maps

Choose an AK Rifle, SMG, Shotgun, Marksman Rifle or Sniper Rifle alongside your pistol. Try the smaller Scrapyard for close encounters, Outpost for fortified cover, Depot for containers and lanes, Riverside for open terrain and crossings, or Neon District for city streets and alleys.

Bots and match settings

Choose Play Solo for a bot match. Start with Easy bots while learning movement and aiming, then adjust the fighter count, difficulty, kill target or match timer in the menu as you become more comfortable.

Devices and beginner tips

Use a desktop browser with WebGL, a keyboard and a mouse for the controls above. Reload behind cover, move between obstacles rather than across open ground, and try short rifle bursts. Change one setting at a time as you learn.
