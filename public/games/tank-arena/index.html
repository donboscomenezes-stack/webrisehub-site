About Tank Arena · How to play

Play Tank Arena – 3D Tank Battles in Your Browser

Play Tank Arena, a free 3D tank browser game with 1v1 through 5v5 battles. Choose a tank and weapon, then fight alongside bot teammates on desktop.

How to play Tank Arena

Choose your tank, weapon and team size before starting a battle. Teams range from 1v1 through 5v5, with bots filling teammate and opponent roles in bot battles. Use the arena layout to approach enemies and keep moving after firing.

Controls and driving styles

Use WASD or arrow keys to drive, the mouse to aim and a mouse click to fire. Number keys 1–6 select weapons. C switches driving styles, Tab shows scores and Escape pauses and displays the controls. Arcade driving points the tank in your chosen screen direction; Classic uses W/S to drive and A/D to turn the hull.

Tanks, weapons and battles

Try the tank and weapon choices in the lobby before changing several settings at once. Learn one driving style first, then compare weapons in smaller bot battles. The game menu shows the available battle options; choose the team size that suits your session.

Desktop requirements and offline status

Tank Arena is currently desktop-only and needs a keyboard, mouse and a browser capable of rendering its 3D arena. Mobile gameplay is not supported. Offline mode is planned and is not available in this release.
